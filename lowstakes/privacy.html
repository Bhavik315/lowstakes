Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

Low Stakes is a free play-money game. You sign in with Google. We never see your Google password, and we do not ask for your phone number or real name. This page says what the site does with information.

Your account

When you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address (and that it is verified). We store, in a database hosted by Supabase: your account id, your email address, the username you choose, your coin balance, and a history of your coin transactions (welcome bonus, bets, winnings, refunds and username-change fees). Your username is shown to other players at your tables. Your email address is not shown to anyone.

Your browser keeps a sign-in session token in local storage so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it. Your coin balance is kept in the database, not in your browser.

To ask for your account and its data to be deleted, contact us using the link at the bottom of this page.

What else stays on your device

Your browser also stores your chip colour, your sound and coach settings, your answer to the 18+ question, whether you accepted the ad notice, and (while you host a table) a snapshot so a reload can resume it. This is kept in your browser's local storage on your device. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Playing with other people

Tables are peer-to-peer. The table's host runs the game in their browser and other players connect to it directly. To set that up, browsers use a public matchmaking service (PeerJS) and STUN and relay (TURN) servers run by third parties such as Google, Cloudflare, PeerJS and Open Relay. These services can see the network addresses involved in making a connection. Other players at your table can also see your network address through the connection. Chat messages and your chosen name are shared with everyone at your table. We do not store them on a server.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, and sign-in and your account data are handled by Supabase and Google. Each of them processes ordinary web requests, including your IP address, to do its job. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Ads

Low Stakes shows no banner or display ads. The only ad on the site is an optional rewarded video: when you run out of chips, you may choose to watch a short ad to refill them. If this feature is switched on, the ad provider may use cookies or device identifiers to deliver and measure the ad. We ask for ads without personalisation. You are shown a notice before your first ad, and you never have to watch one.

Age

Low Stakes is casino-style, so it is for people aged 18 or over. The age question is a self-declaration. We do not verify age and do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we add features that collect more information, this page will be updated first to explain what is collected and why.
