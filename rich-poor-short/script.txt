Why do the rich get richer, and the poor get poorer?
Meet Jake and Marcus. Both earn five thousand dollars a month.
Jake spends first. A new phone at zero percent. A credit card at twenty four percent interest. His money works for the bank.
Marcus invests first. One thousand dollars, every month, into an index fund.
Ten years later, Jake has zero savings and three loans.
Marcus? He put in one hundred twenty thousand dollars, and it grew to two hundred thirty thousand. That is compounding. Money making money, which makes even more money.
And it keeps getting faster. His first fifty thousand dollars took over three years. His latest fifty thousand dollars? Just sixteen months.
So the poor pay interest. The rich collect it.
