Why do the rich get richer, and the poor get poorer?
Meet Rahul and Vikram. Both earn fifty thousand rupees a month.
Rahul spends first. A new phone on EMI. A credit card at thirty six percent interest. His money works for the bank.
Vikram invests first. Twenty thousand rupees, every month, into an index fund.
Ten years later, Rahul has zero savings and three loans.
Vikram? He put in twenty four lakh, and it grew to forty six lakh. That is compounding. Money making money, which makes even more money.
And it keeps getting faster. His first ten lakh took over three years. His latest ten lakh? Just sixteen months.
So the poor pay interest. The rich collect it.
